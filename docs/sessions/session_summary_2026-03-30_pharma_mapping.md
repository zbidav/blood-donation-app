- **ATC additions:** Sugammadex (2015), Remimazolam (2020), Levobupivacaine, Articaine, Esketamine

### Deck 15 — Antihistamines (H1 only)
- 2 families: 1st generation (sedating) and 2nd generation (non-sedating)
- H2 blockers NOT included (covered in deck 16 GI)
- **ATC additions:** Bilastine, Rupatadine

### Deck 16 — GI Drugs
- 15 families: H2 blockers, PPIs, prostaglandins, antacids, mucosal protective, antimotility, 6 laxative sub-families, antimuscarinic
- **Ranitidine (Zantac) withdrawn 2020** — NDMA contamination — noted in XLSX
- **ATC additions:** Vonoprazan/P-CABs (2022), Linaclotide/Plecanatide (GC-C agonists), Naloxegol (PAMORA), Prucalopride

### Deck 17 — Respiratory
- 20 families: SABA, LABA, SAMA, LAMA, methylxanthines, ICS, leukotriene modifiers, mast cell stabilizers, PDE4 inhibitors, LABA+ICS combos, nasal decongestants, antitussives, expectorants, anti-IgE
- Biggest deck this session (41 ingredients)
- **ATC additions:** Ultra-LABA (olodaterol), additional LAMAs (umeclidinium, glycopyrronium), Triple therapy (Trelegy 2017), Anti-IL5 (mepolizumab 2015, benralizumab 2017), Anti-IL4/13 (dupilumab 2018), Anti-TSLP (tezepelumab 2021)

### Deck 18 — Immunosuppressants
- 9 families: CNI, mTOR inhibitors, co-stimulation blockers, purine synthesis inhibitors, anti-T-cell antibodies, anti-IL-2R, glucocorticoids
- Clear signaling pathway (Signal 1/2/3) mapped to drug targets
