## Key Families & ATC Additions This Session

### Deck 21 — Heart Failure (אי ספיקת לב)
- **3 new families:**
  - **Cardiac Glycosides** (C01AA) — Digoxin, Digitoxin; Na+/K+ ATPase inhibition; narrow TI; last-line for systolic HF with EF<40%
  - **β-Adrenergic Agonists (cardiac)** (C01CA) — Dobutamine, Dopamine; IV only; acute decompensated HF
  - **PDE-III Inhibitors (cardiac)** (C01CE) — Milrinone; IV only; increases mortality long-term
- **6 cross-refs:** ACEIs, ARBs, β-Blockers, Aldosterone antagonists, Diuretics, Hydralazine+Nitrates (from decks 19-20)
- **4 ATC additions:**
  - **ARNI** — Sacubitril/Valsartan (Entresto, 2015) — neprilysin inhibitor + ARB; superior to enalapril for HFrEF
  - **Ivabradine** (Corlanor, 2015) — If-channel blocker; HFrEF with HR≥70
  - **SGLT2 Inhibitors** — Dapagliflozin (2020), Empagliflozin (2021); originally diabetes; now HFrEF+HFpEF
  - **Vericiguat** (Verquvo, 2021) — sGC stimulator; worsening chronic HFrEF
- **Key MDA insight:** Many HF drugs (ACEIs, ARBs, β-Blockers, SGLT2i) are dual-indication (HTN vs HF); deferral depends on underlying condition. Entresto/Ivabradine/Vericiguat = clear HF markers.

### Deck 22 — Angina Pectoris (תעוקת לב)
- **2 new families:**
  - **Organic Nitrates & Nitrites** (C01DA) — Nitroglycerin, ISDN, ISMN; NO→cGMP→relaxation; tolerance with continuous use; CONTRAINDICATED with PDE-5 inhibitors
  - **Late Na+ Channel Blocker** (C01EB18) — Ranolazine; add-on for refractory angina; CYP3A4; QT prolongation
- **5 cross-refs:** β-Blockers, CCBs (DHP + non-DHP), Antithrombotics, If-Channel Blocker
- **2 ATC additions:**
  - **P2Y12 context update** — Ticagrelor/Prasugrel for ACS (already in deck 07)
  - **Nicorandil** — K-ATP opener + nitrate; EU/Japan; not FDA-approved
- **Key clinical content:** Slide 22 — drug selection by comorbidity (asthma→avoid β-blockers; diabetes→caution with β-blockers masking hypoglycemia); Slide 24 — treatment algorithm (SL NTG → β-blocker → add CCB or long-acting nitrate → Ranolazine)

### Deck 23 — Cardiac Arrhythmia (קצב לב)
- **6 new families (Vaughan-Williams classification):**
  - **Class IA** — Quinidine, Procainamide, Disopyramide; Na+ block (slow unbinding); atrial+ventricular; Torsades/cinchonism risk
  - **Class IB** — Lidocaine (IV), Mexiletine (PO); Na+ block (fast unbinding); ventricular only; shorter AP; less pro-arrhythmic
  - **Class IC** — Flecainide, Propafenone; Na+ block (very slow); AF without structural disease ONLY; CAST trial: ↑mortality post-MI
  - **Class III** — Sotalol (also β), Amiodarone (multi-channel, most effective), Dronedarone, Ibutilide, Dofetilide; K+ block → prolonged ERP; Torsades risk
  - **Adenosine** — A1 receptor agonist; t½ ~10 sec; first-line acute SVT
  - **Magnesium IV** — membrane stabilizer; first-line for Torsades de Pointes
- **3 cross-refs:** β-Blockers (Class II), CCBs non-DHP (Class IV), Digoxin
- **1 ATC addition:** Vernakalant (Brinavess, 2010 EU) — atrial-selective Na+/K+ blocker; IV AF cardioversion
- **Key clinical content:** Slide 22-24 — arrhythmia-to-drug mapping table; ALL antiarrhythmics can paradoxically cause arrhythmia (pro-arrhythmic effect); Amiodarone now used at low dose with close monitoring

## MDA Relevance Summary (Cardiac Block)

**General principle for cardiac drugs and blood donation:**
- The underlying cardiac condition (IHD, HF, arrhythmia) almost always determines deferral, not the specific drug
- Many drugs serve multiple indications (HTN, HF, angina, arrhythmia) — the recruiter must ask the donor WHY they take the drug
- **Clear HF/IHD markers:** Entresto, Ivabradine, Vericiguat, Ranolazine, Nitroglycerin (SL), Amiodarone, Digoxin
- **Dual-indication ambiguity:** ACEIs, ARBs, β-Blockers, CCBs, SGLT2 inhibitors — could be for HTN alone (potentially eligible) or for cardiac disease (likely permanent deferral)
- **Hospital-only drugs (not relevant for walk-in donors):** Dobutamine, Dopamine, Milrinone, Lidocaine IV, Adenosine, Esmolol, Ibutilide
